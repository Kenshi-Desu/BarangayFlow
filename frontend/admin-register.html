Staff Non-Disclosure Agreement

Barangay Sta. Rita Staff NDA
Olongapo City, Zambales — Effective upon account approval

1. Scope of Confidentiality

All information accessed through BarangayFlow — including resident personal data, contact details, home addresses, request histories, and internal records — is strictly confidential and protected under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

2. Authorized Use Only

Access is granted solely for official barangay business. You must not copy, print, transmit, or disclose any resident data outside of authorized workflows. Unauthorized use constitutes a violation of this agreement and applicable law.

3. Account Security

You are solely responsible for all activity under your account. Never share credentials. Report suspected unauthorized access immediately to the Barangay IT Officer. Sessions are not persisted for security.

4. Document Control

Printed documents are official records. They must not be left unattended, shared with unauthorized persons, or disposed of improperly. Digital exports must only be stored in authorized systems.

5. Consequences of Violation

Violations may result in immediate suspension, administrative disciplinary action, and civil or criminal liability under RA 10173. The Barangay reserves the right to pursue all available legal remedies.
