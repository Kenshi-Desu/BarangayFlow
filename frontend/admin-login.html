Non-Disclosure Agreement

Barangay Sta. Rita Staff NDA
Olongapo City, Zambales — Effective upon login

1. Scope of Confidentiality

All information accessed through BarangayFlow — including but not limited to resident personal data, contact details, home addresses, request histories, health information, and internal barangay records — is strictly confidential and protected under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

2. Authorized Use Only

Access to BarangayFlow is granted solely for official barangay business. Staff must not access, copy, print, transmit, or disclose any resident data outside of authorized workflows. Unauthorized use of data constitutes a violation of this agreement and applicable law.

3. Account Security Obligations

You are solely responsible for all activity performed under your account. You must never share your login credentials with any person. Upon suspicion of unauthorized access, you must immediately report it to the Barangay IT Officer. Sessions are not persisted across devices for security.

4. Data Handling & Document Control

Printed barangay documents must be treated as official records. Physical copies must not be left unattended, shared with unauthorized persons, or disposed of improperly. Digital exports must only be stored in authorized barangay systems.

5. Consequences of Violation

Any violation of this agreement may result in immediate suspension of system access, administrative disciplinary action, and civil or criminal liability under RA 10173 and other applicable Philippine laws. The Barangay Sta. Rita reserves the right to pursue all available legal remedies.

6. Acknowledgement

By checking the acknowledgement box and logging in, you confirm that you have read, understood, and agreed to be bound by this Non-Disclosure Agreement in its entirety.
