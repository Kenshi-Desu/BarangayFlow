Terms & Conditions & Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Welcome to BarangayFlow, a digital barangay management platform operated by the Barangay IT Office. By registering an account, you agree to be bound by these Terms and Conditions and our Privacy Policy. Please read them carefully before proceeding.

2. Eligibility

To register, you must be a legitimate resident of the barangay. You must be at least 18 years old or have the consent of a parent or guardian. You are responsible for providing accurate and up-to-date personal information during registration.

3. Account Responsibilities

You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your account credentials. You must not share your password with anyone. Any activity performed through your account is your responsibility. Report unauthorized access immediately to the barangay IT office.

4. Use of the System

BarangayFlow is intended solely for legitimate barangay service requests and communication. Prohibited activities include submitting false information, misrepresenting your identity, attempting to access other residents' accounts, and any activity that disrupts system operations.

5. Privacy Policy — How We Protect Your Data

What we collect: We collect your full name, email address, phone number, home address, and request history to provide barangay services effectively.

How we use it: Your data is used exclusively to process barangay service requests, notify you of updates, and improve our services. We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties without your consent.

Data security: Your data is stored securely using Firebase (Google Cloud) infrastructure with encryption at rest and in transit. Access is restricted to authorized barangay personnel only.

Your rights: You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data. Contact the barangay IT office to exercise these rights.

Data retention: Your account data is retained for the duration of your residency and for up to 5 years after account closure, in compliance with local government record-keeping requirements.

6. Service Availability

BarangayFlow may experience scheduled or unscheduled downtime for maintenance. The barangay IT office will endeavor to notify users in advance of planned maintenance windows. We are not liable for any inconvenience caused by system unavailability.

7. Account Suspension

The barangay IT office reserves the right to suspend or disable accounts that violate these terms, submit fraudulent requests, or engage in any activity that compromises the integrity of the system.

8. Amendments

These Terms and Conditions may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the system after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised terms. Significant changes will be communicated via the platform.

9. Contact

For questions or concerns about these terms or your personal data, please contact the Barangay IT Office directly or visit the barangay hall during official business hours.
